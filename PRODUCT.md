# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nigerian crypto users (and the diaspora moving value into NGN) who hold, deposit and send
USDC/USDT-style balances and convert to naira through dedicated virtual accounts. They use the
app on a phone, often on mobile data, sometimes in a hurry (paying someone, funding an account
before a trade). Trust matters more than delight here: the KYC step asks for BVN/NIN and a
selfie, so the moment is sensitive. The primary job on the KYC screen is: understand what will
happen, give consent, finish verification in a secure hosted flow, see the result and the new
limits.

## Product Purpose

A wallet that bridges stablecoins and Nigerian naira: deposit on-chain, get a dedicated NGN
virtual account, send and spend in naira with tiered KYC limits. Success looks like a user
completing KYC on the first attempt, understanding what they approved, and landing on a verified
state that clearly shows their Tier 2 limits and account number without contacting support.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, precise. Confidence through restraint: quiet surfaces, one accent, honest
copy, no hype. The interface should feel like a competent banker who respects your time, not a
salesperson and not a carnival.

## Anti-references

- Generic bank KYC forms: blue gradient headers, clip-art shields, stock-photo imagery, filler microcopy.
- Neon crypto bro aesthetic: glowing gradients on black, gradient text, glassy blobs, motion for motion's sake.
- Legacy enterprise: boxy screens where every element sits inside a bordered card with redundant labels.

## Design Principles

1. Earn the ask: explain consent in plain words at the moment it is requested, never before.
2. One decision per screen: the current step is obvious; everything else recedes.
3. Show state, don't hide it: pending, rejected and approved each get honest, specific feedback.
4. Restraint is the brand: one accent color, few borders, generous quiet space; no decoration without meaning.
5. Reassure with facts: real limits, real provider names, real timings instead of adjectives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for text and controls; never state by color alone (icons/labels accompany
status). Support both light and dark app themes via the existing palette system. Respect reduced
motion where possible (keep loops subtle, avoid layout animations). Touch targets ≥ 44pt. Copy
must stay readable for non-native English speakers: short sentences, no jargon (BVN/NIN are
accepted terms of art).
